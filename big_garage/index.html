Big Garage Shortlist

For-sale homes between $300k and $950k with a large detached garage, shop or outbuilding, in the Denver, Boulder and Fort Collins areas and the foothills around them. The garage columns hold only what the listing says; a question mark means it doesn't say. Each home is also scored on its neighborhood with the atlas criteria, and the weights recalculate the scores.

    How to read it

    • Size is the listing's own figure. Square feet are worked out from dimensions where given (30×40 ft is 1,200 sq ft). "Large" means the listing says so without a number. The size column sorts by square feet.
    • 220V is Y when the listing mentions 220 or 240 volt power, and "Power" when it only says the building has electricity. Doors counts overhead doors where stated. RV door is Y for an RV door or a door 12 ft or taller.
    • ADU is "Maybe" where the listing mentions a bunkhouse, studio or office in an outbuilding. None of these homes is on the ADU shortlist.
    • Detached not confirmed marks a few large garages where the listing never says the building is separate from the house.
    • Coverage. This is a capped first pass: 48 searches of listing descriptions and about 100 listing reads, aimed at the foothills and the larger-lot suburbs. Expect misses, especially in dense Denver and in Aurora, Parker and Thornton.
    • Garages under about 600 sq ft were dropped. Redfin does not show the MLS garage tags (220 Volt, Oversized Door, RV Garage), so blanks here mean the listing text is silent, not that the feature is absent. Prices and status are as read on October 9, 2026, mostly from Redfin. County records rarely list outbuildings, so ask the seller for the building's size, power, door heights and permits.
